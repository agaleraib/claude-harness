## 6. Add the ~400 K-session Claude Code research to the README *Why Not Superpowers* evidence list

Key finding for the harness:
> "In a typical session, people make most of the planning decisions (what to do) and Claude makes most of the execution decisions (how to do it). … The greater domain expertise a person brings to a session, the more work Claude does per instruction."

### Why it's relevant
The harness ships `/spec-planner` as a flagship agent whose entire premise is **"discovery questions before code"** — i.e., humans plan, Claude executes.
