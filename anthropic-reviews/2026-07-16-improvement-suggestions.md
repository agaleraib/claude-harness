### Why it's relevant

This is **exactly** the architecture Anthropic's May 30 post describes: sandbox lane = OS is the boundary (matches sandcastle); native lane = OS is NOT the boundary, so layered guardrails compensate (matches worktree). The post's central lesson — "as capability grows, containment must escalate from lightweight isolation to hardened VMs" — corroborates the harness's default-sandcastle-with-explicit-worktree-opt-in posture.
