## 2. Consider adding Fable 5 as an optional escalation tier for `stakes.level: high` in orchestrator routing

**Source:**
- [Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 — Anthropic News, 2026-06-09](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5)
- [Redeploying Claude Fable 5 — Anthropic News, ~2026-06-30](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5) — 19-day global rollback (2026-06-12 → 2026-07-01) after a jailbreak that produced exploit-demonstration code; redeploy shipped with a classifier that blocks the bypass in >99% of cases and Anthropic doubled its safety-research staff before relaunch.

### Why it's (potentially) relevant
Fable 5 is Anthropic's first publicly-available **Mythos-class** model — a tier above Opus (Fable is priced at $10 / $50 per M tokens, roughly 2× Opus 4.8 / 5). Public reporting has it leading agentic-coding evals (~80% SWE-Bench Pro). The harness's orchestrator retry-ladder (`.claude/agents/orchestrator.md:248, 253, 256, 380`) currently ceilings at "opus-4.7/xhigh" — after which the orchestrator gives up. In a post-Opus-5 world, the same ladder ceiling would be `claude-opus-5/xhigh`. Fable 5 offers a natural next rung: if `stakes.level: high` is set and a task fails at Opus/xhigh, try Fable 5 before surfacing the failure to the human.

### Why this is `defer`, not `apply` or even `spec`

1. **The harness has no `stakes.level: high` consumer today.** `.harness-profile:18` sets `stakes.level: medium`. No consumer project of this harness that I can grep for has `stakes.level: high`. The escalation tier would be untested infrastructure serving a hypothetical caller.
2. **Fable 5 is unstable at the platform layer.** Global rollback for 19 days is a serious operational-availability event. A harness that pins Fable 5 as an escalation tier during another rollback would silently degrade to "we tried the top tier and got a 404". The redeploy classifier restrictions may also affect coding tasks (the trigger was a Fable-5 exploit-demo); Anthropic explicitly said "some routine tasks like coding" would be blocked at redeploy time — the current shape is unclear.
3. **2× cost multiplier without a first-party ROI curve.** Anthropic hasn't published a "Fable-5 vs Opus-5 on agentic coding" benchmark that would let the harness say "for hardest tasks, Fable 5 is worth 2× the cost". Third-party numbers exist but aren't first-party.
4. **Overlaps with retry policy, not model pinning.** Even if adopted, this belongs in the orchestrator's retry ladder (`.claude/agents/orchestrator.md:248-266`), not the `.harness-profile` model pins. The routing rule would be conditional (only fires when `stakes.level: high` AND primary retry exhausts). That's a Wave 2 addition, not a model-pin change.
5. **Speculative for solo tooling.** Per `anthropic-reviews/README.md`'s "apply / defer / reject / spec" rubric: "speculative for solo tooling" is a defer indicator, not a reject indicator — flag it for the maintainer rather than kill the idea outright.

### Concrete change (deferred)

None this run. When/if the harness gains a `stakes.level: high` consumer AND Fable 5 stabilizes for a quarter:

1. Extend the orchestrator retry ladder to include one more rung: `opus/xhigh → fable/xhigh` (only when `.harness-profile` has `stakes.level: high` AND `enable_fable_escalation: true` opt-in).
2. Add the flag to `.harness-profile` schema in `skills/project-init/SKILL.md`.
3. Emit a Surface B JSONL escalation event that names the model transition and the cost multiplier so the maintainer can retroactively audit "did the Fable escalation actually save the task?"
4. Document the availability risk explicitly in `README.md`: Fable-tier is opt-in because it's more expensive and has shown platform-availability volatility.

### Expected payoff (when applied)
For consumer projects with `stakes.level: high` and hard-to-solve tasks (a real one appeared with `.claude` self-hosted enterprise deployments), the orchestrator has one more rung before surfacing failure — potentially saving a human round-trip on the hardest 5% of tasks.

### Verify before applying
1. `grep -rn "stakes.level.*high\|stakes:.*level:.*high" /home/user/claude-harness` — must return at least one consumer project (not just the harness's own `.harness-profile` which is `medium`) before this becomes non-hypothetical.
2. `grep -rn "fable\|Fable\|mythos\|Mythos" /home/user/claude-harness` — must be zero in the orchestrator + profile paths today (bookkeeping mentions in `anthropic-reviews/` files don't count). If Fable is already wired in, this § is stale.
3. Confirm Fable 5 has been GA for at least one quarter with no further global rollbacks. Check [Anthropic status page](https://status.anthropic.com) or first-party post-redeploy statement.
4. Confirm the Redeploying Fable 5 post's "routine coding tasks may be blocked" caveat has been resolved — a classifier restriction on coding-adjacent tasks would defeat the purpose of an escalation tier.

**Recommended verdict:** defer until a `stakes.level: high` consumer project exists AND Fable 5 stabilizes — speculative for solo tooling; no downstream caller today; model itself has shown 19-day-rollback-scale instability; cost is 2× without a first-party ROI curve. Flag for maintainer awareness.
